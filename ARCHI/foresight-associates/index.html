Studio Introduction

Contemporary Architecture, Timeless Indian Sensibility.

Based in Vadodara (Baroda), Gujarat, Foresight Associates is led by principal architects and designers Shivali Agrawal, Kunal Patel, and Azaz Saleh. We craft bespoke residential and commercial environments that celebrate spatial freedom and tactile luxury.

"Design is not simply about creating spaces. It is about shaping experiences, emotions and everyday living."

Studio Philosophy • Foresight Associates

Every residence, villa, and workspace we design emerges from an intimate dialogue with its context. By layering raw textures—exposed brick, tactile concrete, warm teakwood, and custom metal jalis—with abundant natural daylight and biophilic pockets, we create architectural narratives that age with grace.
