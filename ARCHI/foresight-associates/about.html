About The Studio

Rooted in Context.
Driven by Artistry.

Foresight Associates is an architectural and interior design praxis based in Vadodara, Gujarat. We create contemporary sanctuaries where raw materiality, daylight, and nature converge into harmonious everyday living.

Studio Origins

Spatial Authenticity in the Heart of Gujarat

Founded and anchored in Vadodara, Foresight Associates was born out of a collective impulse: to build architecture and interiors that are unapologetically authentic to their regional landscape, yet sophisticated enough to resonate globally.

In our work, luxury is never measured in superficial ornamentation or ephemeral trends. True luxury is defined by the honesty of materials—tactile raw concrete, warm clay bricks, local Kota stone, unembellished teakwood, and the interplay of filtered sunlight against textured plaster.

Over years of dedicated practice across Gujarat, our studio has shaped a recognized portfolio of expansive family villas, modern-rustic suburban homes, corporate workspaces, and bespoke show apartments, published widely on national architectural platforms like The Architect’s Diary and Volume Zero.

Vadodara Studio Praxis
Guiding Philosophy

"Design is not simply about creating spaces. It is about shaping experiences, emotions and everyday living."

This foundational conviction governs every pencil sketch, material mock-up, and structural coordinate produced in our Vadodara studio.

01

Material Honesty

We allow raw textures—exposed concrete, rustic brick, patinated metals, and warm timber—to speak for themselves, celebrating natural imperfections that deepen in beauty over time.

02

Biophilic Integration

Architecture and vegetation must breathe together. From indoor open-sky plant courtyards to tranquil water bodies, our designs draw nature into the intimate core of everyday life.

03

Daylight Choreography

Sunlight in Gujarat is a living material. Through fluted glass screens, custom perforated corten jalis, and east-facing light wells, we orchestrate soft daylight without uncomfortable thermal heat.

Our Methodology

The Architectural Approach

Every commission begins with immersive listening. We study how our clients move, gather, work, and seek quietude, transforming personal daily rituals into enduring architectural form.

01. Context & Dialogue

Deep study of site geography, sun orientation, wind corridors, and family rhythms before drafting the initial spatial volumes.

02. Spatial Choreography

Sculpting fluid transitions between public reception salons, private sanctuaries, internal courtyards, and vertical light axes.

03. Material & Artisanal Detailing

Tactile material boards, bespoke jali patterns, hand-finished wall plasters, and collaborations with master craftspeople.

04. Artistic Execution & Realisation

Rigorous on-site supervision ensuring every joint, reveal, and fixture adheres strictly to the studio’s standard of craftsmanship.

Studio Leadership

The Principal Designers

Foresight Associates is guided by its three founding principal designers, uniting distinct architectural perspectives into a cohesive, award-recognized spatial language.

Shivali Agrawal

Principal Designer Spatial Harmony & Residential Sanctuaries

With a deep commitment to tactile luxury and spatial harmony, Shivali spearheads the conceptual and aesthetic direction across the firm’s signature residential commissions. Principal Designer
Founding Partner Vadodara

Kunal Patel

Principal Designer Structural Clarity & Facade Architecture

Kunal steers the studio’s architectural discipline, emphasizing structural clarity, contextual elevation design, and climate-conscious spatial forms. Known for his work on modern-rustic villas and commercial site offices, he explores exposed concrete, raw brickwork, and custom corten steel installations that interact with Gujarat's sun.

Principal Designer

Azaz brings meticulous detail to spatial planning, bespoke furniture systems, and technical execution. Core Standards

Studio Values

Authenticity of Place

Every structure belongs genuinely to Vadodara and its climatic conditions—respecting natural breeze channels, seasonal sunlight angles, and regional building traditions.

Longevity Over Trends

We deliberately resist short-lived decorative fads. By grounding our work in monolithic forms, raw textures, and proportion, our architecture remains dignified across decades.

Bespoke Craftsmanship

From customized perforated jalis to handcrafted teakwood credenzas, we collaborate closely with local artisans, honoring the human hand behind every architectural element.
